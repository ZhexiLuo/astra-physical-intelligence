03 / HOW WE BUILT ITFOUR EXPLICIT STEPS

The useful part
is the chain between them.

Video informs the task. Geometry defines contact.
The renderer follows the recorded physics.

  1. 01

    Read the task.

    OBSERVE

    Identify the hold, sweep, and withdrawal in six seconds of video. Make scale and occlusion assumptions explicit.

    RGB → task & dimensions
  2. 02

    Build the world.

    MODEL

    Give the dustpan an open cavity and the brush separate contact and visual geometry. Keep their coordinate frames aligned.

    Scene spec → MJCF & assets
  3. 03

    Let contact work.

    EXECUTE

    A scripted skill and IK targets drive TIAGo++. Actuator controls advance the robot; real contact moves six free blocks.

    ctrl + mj_step → state & contact
  4. 04

    Make it inspectable.

    RENDER

    Export the trajectory with MuJoCo’s USDExporter. Bake it into Blender, add the visual tools, and mount three cameras.

    USD → Blender & three views
A REAL ROBOT MODEL

Two arms.
Original grippers.

TIAGo++ is a commercial PAL Robotics platform. We use its official MuJoCo Menagerie model under Apache-2.0, retaining the robot geometry, arm joints, and force limits. For this task, the wheeled base is fixed and the tools start rigidly attached at the original parallel grippers.Explore the pinned robot model ↗

The assumptions that make this result specific+

Estimated scaleDimensions, mass, and friction are modeling choices. The single camera was not metrically calibrated.

Tools already heldThe base is fixed and the tools are rigidly attached. The task starts at the tool-use stage.

Rigid bristle proxyContact uses rigid geometry. Individual Blender filaments supply the brush’s visual appearance.

Six blocks from the startThree are clearly visible initially in the source, and six at the end. The simulation conserves six free bodies throughout.

Scripted controlThe video informs a hand-written skill and an IK controller. No policy training or end-to-end imitation learning is involved.

One recorded trajectoryAll three renders and the interactive viewer follow the saved rollout. Visual reconstruction supplies appearance.
